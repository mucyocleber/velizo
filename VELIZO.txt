Preface
Introduction
VELIZO is a modern, AI-powered international recruitment platform designed to connect global employers with exceptional talent through a secure, intelligent, and scalable digital ecosystem.
The platform streamlines international hiring by bringing together candidates, employers, recruitment agencies, and administrators within a single cloud-based environment powered by Artificial Intelligence.
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) serves as the official technical blueprint for the design, development, implementation, testing, deployment, and long-term maintenance of the VELIZO platform.
The document provides a comprehensive description of the project's business objectives, functional requirements, artificial intelligence capabilities, system architecture, database design, implementation strategy, security requirements, and overall technical direction.

This document represents the agreed technical foundation upon which the VELIZO platform will be designed, developed, and continuously enhanced.

Chapter 1 – Executive Summary & Business Analysis
1.1 Executive Summary
The global recruitment industry is evolving rapidly, creating a growing demand for secure, intelligent, and efficient hiring solutions. VELIZO is designed to address this need by providing an AI-powered international recruitment platform that connects global employers with exceptional talent through a modern, scalable, and cloud-based ecosystem.
Rather than operating as a traditional job board, VELIZO integrates recruitment management, candidate services, Artificial Intelligence, Career Passport, document verification, and employer tools into a unified platform that simplifies international hiring while improving efficiency, transparency, and trust.
________________________________________
1.2 Business Vision
To become a trusted global recruitment platform that connects employers with exceptional talent through innovative technology and intelligent recruitment solutions.
________________________________________
1.3 Mission Statement
To simplify international recruitment by delivering a secure, AI-driven platform that empowers employers to hire qualified professionals efficiently while enabling candidates to build successful global careers.
________________________________________
1.4 Project Purpose
The purpose of VELIZO is to centralize and modernize the international recruitment process by integrating candidate management, employer services, document verification, AI assistance, communication, and analytics into a single digital ecosystem.
________________________________________
1.5 Business Challenges
The current recruitment market faces several common challenges:
•	Difficulty identifying verified international job opportunities.
•	Large volumes of unqualified job applications.
•	Limited candidate verification and document validation.
•	Fragmented recruitment workflows.
•	Basic search systems with poor matching accuracy.
•	Limited AI-powered career guidance.
•	Time-consuming recruitment processes.
These challenges reduce recruitment efficiency, increase operational costs, and negatively impact hiring outcomes.
________________________________________
1.6 Proposed Solution
VELIZO addresses these challenges through a comprehensive AI-powered recruitment ecosystem featuring:
•	Intelligent job discovery
•	AI-powered search and recommendations
•	Career Passport
•	Secure document management
•	Resume management
•	Employer recruitment tools
•	Recruitment agency management
•	AI Career Assistant
•	Recruitment analytics
•	Secure cloud infrastructure
The platform is designed to support both current business requirements and future global expansion.
